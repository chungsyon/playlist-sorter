Playlist Sorter

Connect YouTube Music

This app talks to YouTube Music using your browser session. Credentials stay on this Mac in browser.json and last about two years.

How to get your headers
  1. Open music.youtube.com while logged in.
  2. Open DevTools (⌥⌘I) and go to the Network tab.
  3. Type /browse in the filter box, then click around the page to make a request appear.
  4. Click a POST request → Headers → copy everything under Request Headers.
  5. Paste it below. The Cookie line must be included.

What are we sorting?

Destination playlists

Tick the playlists songs should be routed into, then describe each one in a sentence. These descriptions are the single biggest accuracy lever — "Slow Down" means nothing on its own, but "mellow, low-tempo songs for winding down at the end of the day" gives the model something real to match against.
