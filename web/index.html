Playlist Sorter

Step 01

Connect YouTube Music

This app signs in with your existing browser session. Nothing is uploaded — credentials are written to browser.json on this Mac and stay valid for about two years.

Getting your headers

  1. Open music.youtube.com while logged in.
  2. Open DevTools with ⌥⌘I and select the Network tab.
  3. Filter for browse, then click a playlist in the sidebar so a request appears.
  4. Open a POST request, go to Headers, and copy everything under Request Headers.
  5. Paste it opposite. The Cookie line has to be included.

Copy the headers, not the request URL. That mix-up is what produces the Expecting value: line 1 column 1 error.

Step 02

What are we sorting?

Choose the playlist to read from, then pick where its songs are allowed to go.

Destination playlists

Tick every playlist songs may be routed into. A song can land in more than one.

Descriptions are the single biggest accuracy lever. "Slow Down" means nothing on its own — "mellow, low-tempo songs for winding down at the end of the day" gives the model something real to match against.

Step 03

Sorting

Songs go to the model fifty at a time. Pause whenever you like — the job resumes from its cursor, and nothing already classified is re-sent.
